Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need riding experience?

Yes. You should be comfortable riding a 350 cc bike on hilly roads. Beginners can ride pillion with a road captain.

Do I need a permit?

Yes, an Inner Line Permit is needed for this route. We guide you through the process, and you only need to carry your ID proof.

When is the best time to ride?

Usually May to June and September to October. Roads can close during heavy monsoon rain.

What if the bike breaks down?

A mechanic and backup vehicle travel with the group, so you can continue your ride quickly.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Reschedule requests are free up to 15 days before departure. Add your own refund policy here.
